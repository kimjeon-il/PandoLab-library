# Atrek sample scope correction

Report correction completed 2026-11-04. Geometry: not changed by this correction. Original samples.json, metrics.json, figures and extraction methods remain unchanged.

The original ussr-atrek result measured a window [54.3,37.3,55.2,38.3], selected the longest of two compiled shared-boundary components, and contained 17 points over 36.2924646924979 km (0.4684167951677889 vertices/km). It did not measure the complete approved 140-point replacement. No 299 km result appears in the original report. Applying that partial-sample density to all 140 points is an invalid extrapolation and produced the later mistaken approximate 299 km description. This was a scope/interpretation error, not a projection or arithmetic error in the original stored sample.

The original 16 sample edges are all contained in unchanged modern NE IRN/TKM boundary and also within the approved replacement's retained-modern tail. They are therefore not evidence that the newly traced historical river is already comparable in detail. The earlier no-excess-detail finding applies only to the measured retained component, not the entire historical Atrek replacement or all historical borders.

A fresh selected-source/compiled check at data a548d5fa6612a97f998b7bdb326a27eb8b8d9717 found all 139 edges of the 140-point approved replacement present in USSR source and compiled geometry. Its complete path is approximately 100.056 km in a local WGS84 AEQD projection. This contains three roles: explicit existing-coast closure (2 edges,3 points,1.650 km), nonmodern approved river trace (120 edges,121 points,61.624 km), and exact modern NE reuse (17 edges,18 points,36.782 km). Shared endpoints explain why role vertex counts cannot simply be added. The comparison current NE river context in the same nonmodern-river extent is 52.088 km/13 points; densities are 1.964 versus0.250 vertices/km. Routes/endpoints differ politically and physically, so this is a detail diagnostic, not positional error, a legally reconstructed river or a ready-made tolerance.

The closure is preserved separately. Dense historical river review remains open; no new river simplification, source replacement or runtime publication is claimed by this correction.
